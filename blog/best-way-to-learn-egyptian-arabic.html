Short answer: If you are learning Arabic to talk with an Egyptian partner, spouse or in-laws, learn spoken Egyptian Arabic (Masri), not Modern Standard Arabic (MSA). Start with the phrases your family uses at home, learn which ones are polite enough for elders, and practice in short, daily sessions. Why Egyptian Arabic and not Modern Standard Arabic?

Modern Standard Arabic is the formal Arabic of news broadcasts, books and official documents. Egyptian families do not speak it at the dinner table. At home, at weddings, on the phone with your mother-in-law, people speak Egyptian Arabic, also called Masri.

That means many learners who start with an MSA course end up understanding the news better than their own in-laws. If your goal is family conversation, start with Masri.

What is the best way to learn Egyptian Arabic to talk with my partner's family?

Focus on spoken Egyptian Arabic (Masri), not MSA. Learn home and family phrases first, learn which phrases are respectful enough for elders and in-laws, and practice for a few minutes every day. Ask your partner to teach you the exact phrases their family uses. SpeakMasri's Partner Phrases feature lets your partner add those phrases straight into your lessons.

Is there an app to learn spoken Egyptian Arabic for my Egyptian husband's or wife's family?

Yes. SpeakMasri is a free web app that teaches spoken Egyptian Arabic specifically for partners, spouses and in-laws. Phrases are sorted by register, so you know what is right for your partner versus your mother-in-law.

How do I learn spoken Egyptian Arabic instead of Modern Standard Arabic?

Choose resources that say they teach Egyptian Arabic or Masri, not just "Arabic." Most general Arabic courses teach MSA. Learn from audio, so you hear how Egyptians actually pronounce words, and practice with Egyptian speakers. SpeakMasri teaches only spoken Masri.

How do you say "I love you" in Egyptian Arabic?

In spoken Egyptian Arabic, "I love you" is بحبك. Say "bahebak" to a man and "bahebik" to a woman. It is an intimate phrase, for your partner. Full guide: How to Say "I Love You" in Egyptian Arabic.

Do I need to know any Arabic before starting?

No prior Arabic is required. SpeakMasri is designed for complete beginners who may not read Arabic script yet. Masri is written in ways that are easy to sound out, and you can build from there at your own pace.
