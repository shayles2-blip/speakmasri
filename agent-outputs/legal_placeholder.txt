SpeakMasri (“SpeakMasri,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a small web app made and operated by a single independent developer. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1) Information we collect
A) Account information (Firebase Authentication)
- Name (if you provide one)
- Email address
- Password (handled by Firebase Authentication). We do not store your raw password ourselves.

B) Learning data (Firebase Firestore)
- Lesson progress, XP, milestones, completion history
- Any custom phrases or notes you choose to save in the app

C) Basic analytics (Firebase Analytics)
We collect basic usage events to understand how the app is used, such as:
- Sign-ups
- Lesson completion
- Return visits / session activity
This is used to improve the app. We do not use ad networks or third‑party advertising trackers.

2) Audio and Text-to-Speech
SpeakMasri uses Google Cloud Text-to-Speech to generate pronunciation audio. We send the text needed to generate the audio to this service.
- We do not collect voice recordings from you.
- We do not ask for or store microphone audio.

3) How we use information
We use your information to:
- Create and manage your account
- Save your learning progress and show it back to you
- Provide the core learning features (including generating audio)
- Understand basic usage trends and improve the app
- Respond to support requests

4) What we do NOT do
- We do not sell your personal data.
- We do not run ads or share your data with ad networks.
- We do not use “third-party trackers” beyond the services described above (Firebase and Google Cloud used to run the app).

5) Sharing and service providers
We use these service providers to operate SpeakMasri:
- Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics)
- Google Cloud Text-to-Speech
These providers process data on our behalf so the app can function.

6) Data retention
We keep your account information and learning data while your account is active. We may retain limited logs/records as needed for security, debugging, and legal compliance.

7) Account deletion
You can request deletion of your account and associated learning data by emailing: support@speakmasri.com
We may ask you to verify ownership of the account (for example, by emailing us from the account’s email address). Deletion may take a reasonable amount of time to complete.

8) Security
We use standard security measures provided by our infrastructure (Firebase/Google Cloud) and reasonable precautions to protect your data. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

9) Children
SpeakMasri is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10) Changes
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and may provide additional notice in the app or on the website.

11) Contact
Questions or requests: support@speakmasri.com
