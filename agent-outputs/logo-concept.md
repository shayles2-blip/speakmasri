1. **Ta’meya Wrap** (🥙)  
The ubiquitous Egyptian falafel sandwich, eaten at any hour, from street carts to family picnics. Better than a teapot because it instantly signals casual, contemporary Egyptian life and shared, everyday joy — no tourist lens needed.

2. **Ful Pot** (🍲)  
Egypt’s national dish, ful medames: slow-simmered fava beans, a humble staple of family breakfasts and baladi warmth. Better than a teapot because it embodies the comfort of a real home kitchen, not a curated hospitality scene.

3. **Egyptian Flag** (🇪🇬)  
The modern national tricolor with the Eagle of Saladin, clean and instantly recognizable. Better than a teapot because it directly, unapologetically declares “Egypt” without leaning on any exotic shorthand, grounding the app in real national identity today.
