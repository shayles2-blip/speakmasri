**Incorrect:** border `#C1652E`, background `#FDF1EA`, and a 380ms `shakeX` (`translateX: 0,-8,7,-5,3,0`). Card gets a 2px inset ring that pulses once. The correct answer's card then does a slow 600ms `#D4AF37` glow-in. `navigator.vibrate([15,40,15])`.

**Both:** a full-width 3px top "pulse line" sweeps left→right in the answer color over 500ms — a visual "sound".
